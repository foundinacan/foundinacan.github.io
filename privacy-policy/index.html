1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how NEALFUN INC. ("neal.fun," "we," "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use our services. NEALFUN INC. is a Virginia corporation with offices in New York, USA, and is the data controller for the information described in this policy.

This policy applies to:

  • Our website at neal.fun, including all games and projects hosted on it
  • Our mobile applications for iOS, iPadOS and Android, including Infinite Craft and Unusual Suspects

Together, these are our "Services." Our Services can be used without creating an account. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at [email protected].

2. Information we collect

Information collected automatically. When you use our Services, we and our advertising partners may automatically collect:

  • Device and technical information: device type, operating system, browser type, screen size and language settings
  • Approximate location: country or city-level location derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise location
  • Usage information: pages and games visited, time spent, interactions with the Services, and crash and error reports
  • Identifiers: cookies and similar technologies on our website, and mobile advertising identifiers in our apps (Apple's IDFA, only where you permit tracking, and Google's Advertising ID on Android)
  • Privacy preferences: your consent and opt-out choices, so we can respect them

Information stored on your device. Most games store progress, settings and creations locally in your browser or on your device. Some features, such as Infinite Craft's daily challenges and discoveries, send gameplay data to our servers so they can work.

Information you provide. If you sign up for email updates about new posts, we collect your email address. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

Information we do not collect. We do not require accounts to use our Services and do not collect contacts, photos or precise location. Where our apps offer in-app purchases, payments are processed by Apple or Google, and we do not receive your payment card details.

Unusual Suspects for iOS

The Unusual Suspects app for iPhone and iPad collects and shares the following information:

  • Sketches you draw. When you submit a sketch, the drawing and the name of the suspect are sent to our servers so the court can judge it. Sketches are not stored after the verdict is returned.
  • Advertising. The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location, and information about how you interact with ads, as described in Google's privacy policy. If you allow tracking when the app asks, ads can be personalized. In the European Economic Area and the UK, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from the "Privacy Options" entry in the app's menu. Elsewhere, you can limit personalization from your device's privacy settings.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes or encounters an error, an anonymous report with technical details about the failure and your device is sent to Sentry so we can fix it. Reports contain no personal information or sketches.
  • iCloud. Your filed cases are stored in your own iCloud account so they stay in sync across your devices. We have no access to them. "Reset Progress" in the app's menu deletes them from all your devices.

Where the law of the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland applies, we rely on your consent for personalized advertising and for accessing information on your device, on our legitimate interests for operating, securing and improving the Services and for non-personalized advertising where permitted, and on legal obligations where applicable.

5. Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics to understand how our website is used. Plausible does not use cookies, does not track users across websites and does not collect personal information. It provides us with aggregated statistics only.

6. Mobile apps

Our mobile apps use Google AdMob to display ads.

  • iOS and iPadOS: before any tracking occurs, our apps display a brief explanation followed by Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track," your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared, and you will see non-personalized ads.
  • Android: our advertising partners may use your device's Google Advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in your device settings.
  • Permissions: our apps do not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

For app-specific details, see "Unusual Suspects for iOS" in Section 2.

To exercise these rights, email [email protected].
